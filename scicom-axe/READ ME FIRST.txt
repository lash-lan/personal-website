SCICOM AXE
==========

Everything is in this folder. There is nothing to download and nothing to
install except Node.js, once.


STEP 1 — Node.js (once only)
----------------------------

Open https://nodejs.org and download the big green LTS button. Run the
installer and click Next until it finishes. Restart the computer afterwards.

If you already have Node.js, skip this.


STEP 2 — Put this folder somewhere sensible
-------------------------------------------

Unzip it, then drag the "scicom-axe" folder to your Documents folder, or
wherever you will remember it. Do not leave it inside the Downloads folder or
inside the zip file itself — Windows will not run it properly from there.


STEP 3 — Start it
-----------------

Open the folder and double-click the one for your computer:

    Windows        Start Scicom Axe.cmd
    Mac or Linux   Start Scicom Axe.command

A black window opens and stays open. That IS the system running. Your browser
opens by itself a second later showing the dashboard.

Leave the black window alone while you are using it. Close it when you have
finished.


FIRST THING TO DO — CHECK YOUR NAME
-----------------------------------

Click "Settings" in the list down the left. Your name, your job title and the
person who signs off at second level (Shefeeque Abdul Rahman) are written into
every form automatically, so you never type them twice. Correct anything that
is wrong and press Save.


TO GIVE IT AN INVOICE OR A REPORT
---------------------------------

Click "Upload a file" in the list down the left, and drop in a PDF invoice, an
Excel or CSV sheet, or a Word report. It reads what it can and shows you what
it thinks should be recorded. NOTHING IS SAVED until you have looked at it and
pressed the button.

A photograph of a receipt is kept but not read — the words in a photo are a
picture, and reading them needs software this does not install. The boxes come
up blank for you to type in.


TO USE IT ON YOUR PHONE
-----------------------

Double-click "Start Scicom Axe - phone.cmd" (Windows) or
"Start Scicom Axe - phone.command" (Mac) instead. It prints a QR code —
point your phone camera at it. Your phone must be on the same Wi-Fi as this
laptop, and the laptop must stay switched on with that window open.

There is no password on it. Anyone else on the same Wi-Fi who scans it can see
your tasks, the policies and the finance figures. Fine at home. Think first on
the office network.


IF THE WINDOW FLASHES AND DISAPPEARS
------------------------------------

Node.js is not installed, or the computer cannot see it yet. Do step 1, restart
the computer, and try again.

ON A MAC, IF IT REFUSES TO OPEN
-------------------------------

macOS blocks downloaded scripts the first time. Right-click the file, choose
Open, then click Open again in the box that appears. You only do this once.
