# The named characters still needing art

Twenty five of them. Eleven have nothing at all, and fourteen are borrowing a
painting from somewhere else on the site, which works but is not theirs.

Paste section 1 first, then one character per message in the same conversation.

---

## 1. The house style - paste this first

```
For this whole conversation you are making character art for a dark fantasy
trading card game called Blood of Icetear. Every image must follow these rules
without being reminded.

FORMAT
- 2:3 portrait, 1024 x 1536 or larger. Never square. Never landscape.

COMPOSITION - the card frame covers parts of the image, so:
- Put the face in the upper-middle third. NEVER at the very top edge.
- Leave a clear hand's width of empty space ABOVE any crown, helm, horns,
  antlers or halo. This is the rule that gets broken most often.
- Put nothing that matters in the bottom quarter. It will be covered.
- Waist-up or chest-up. No full-body-at-a-distance shots.
- Keep hands, weapons and wings inside the frame, away from the left and
  right edges.

STYLE
- Painted, not photographic. Smooth, confident paint. Soft edges.
- Dark, rich, low-key colour. One strong light source.
- Never a plain white, grey or empty studio background. Every character
  stands somewhere real, with depth and atmosphere behind them.
- Period-accurate: armour, cloth, leather, fur. No modern clothing, no
  modern hair, no studio portrait lighting.
- It must read instantly at thumbnail size: clear silhouette, strong shape.

RENDER IT SIMPLY - this is the rule you are most likely to break
- Large, simple shapes. Big areas of flat or softly blended colour.
- Sharp detail on the FACE and one other thing only. Soften everything else.
- Throw the background out of focus.
- NO speckling, stippling, grain, noise, scattered dots or spatter.
- NO fine repeating micro-texture crawling over armour, cloth, skin or
  foliage. A surface may be plain.
- Do not add straps, buckles, rivets, studs, chains, beads, embroidery or
  filigree that I did not ask for.
- When in doubt, render less. An over-detailed image turns to mush at the
  size these are actually seen.

NEVER
- No text, letters, numbers, signatures, watermarks or borders.
- No card frame, no UI, no nameplate. Art only, edge to edge.

Reply "ready" and I will send the first character.
```

belong to the same order: the same robes and the same instruments, four
different peoples. Do these four together so they match.
