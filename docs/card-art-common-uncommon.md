# Common and Uncommon card art

Fifty three cards: twenty five Common, twenty eight Uncommon. These are the
rank and file, the cards you actually play on turns one to three.

**The one thing to get right:** these are a *kind* of person, not a person.
An ordinary Red Ranger, not Shaedra. Plain gear, no grandeur, nothing
heroic. If one of them looks like a Legendary character the set stops having
a shape.

## How to use this

Paste **section 1** as your first message in a ChatGPT conversation. Then work
through one group at a time, one card per message, staying in the same
conversation for the whole group so the faction looks like a faction.

Save each as `public/images/cards/` plus the filename given. Tell me when a
batch is done and I will import them.

# 1. Paste this first

```
For this whole conversation you are making character art for a dark fantasy
trading card game called Blood of Icetear. Every image must follow these
rules without being reminded.

FORMAT
- 2:3 portrait, 1024 x 1536 or larger. Never square. Never landscape.

COMPOSITION - the card frame covers parts of the image, so:
- Put the face in the upper-middle third. NEVER at the very top edge.
- Leave a clear hand's width of empty space ABOVE any crown, helm, horns,
  antlers or halo. This is the rule that gets broken most often.
- Put nothing that matters in the bottom quarter. It will be covered.
- Waist-up or chest-up. No full-body-at-a-distance shots.
- Keep hands, weapons and wings inside the frame, away from the left and
  right edges.

STYLE
- Painted, not photographic. Oil and digital painting, visible brushwork.
- Dark, rich, low-key colour. One strong light source.
- Never a plain white, grey or empty studio background. Every character
  stands somewhere real, with depth and atmosphere behind them.
- Period-accurate: armour, cloth, leather, fur. No modern clothing, no
  modern hair, no studio portrait lighting.
- It must read instantly at thumbnail size: clear silhouette, strong shape.

THESE ARE COMMON SOLDIERS
Everything in this conversation is rank and file: a type of person, not a
named hero. Worn, practical, mass-issue gear. Mud, repairs, mismatched kit.
No ornate armour, no glowing weapons, no crowns, no dramatic hero poses.
They should look like people who do this for a living and are tired.

NEVER
- No text, letters, numbers, signatures, watermarks or borders.
- No card frame, no UI, no nameplate. Art only, edge to edge.

Reply "ready" and I will send the first one.
```

# 2. The Red Rangers (6)

All in the same deep green forest of enormous trees with crimson banners
hanging between them, light coming down in shafts. Crimson and blackened
steel. Do these six in one sitting.

**Red Ranger** - `red-ranger.webp`
```
A rank and file Red Ranger: a young high elf soldier in plain crimson leather
and a blackened steel breastplate, a shortbow over the shoulder, hood down.
```
